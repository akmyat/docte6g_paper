\section{Experiment Part II: NR Application, Propagation, and Energy Evaluation}
\label{sec:exp2-nr-energy}

Having characterized the runtime performance of the embedded approach, we evaluate the Sionna~RT backend in a realistic 3GPP~NR deployment \cite{5glena,tr38901}.
This section assesses application-level KPIs (throughput, packet loss, delay, jitter), propagation-level KPIs (path loss, path delay, CQI, MCS), and energy consumption across three radio environments — free space, urban macro, and urban micro — and across gNB antenna array sizes of $2\times2$, $4\times4$, and $8\times8$, under low, medium, and high traffic loads.
