\subsection{Stochastic Channel Models in NR Simulation}
The 3GPP TR~38.901 geometry-based stochastic model is the de facto standard for system-level NR evaluation, providing path-loss, shadowing, and multipath cluster models for urban macro, urban micro, and indoor environments across sub-6~GHz and mmWave bands \cite{tr38901}.
This model is integrated directly into ns-3 and 5G-LENA, enabling large-scale multi-cell studies without a geometric scene description \cite{ns3,5glena}.

Stochastic models are well suited for average-case performance analysis but generate independent channel realizations per link and per simulation run.
They cannot reproduce the spatial correlation of multipath components as a user moves through a specific deployment, nor can they resolve the site-specific angles and delays required for evaluating ISAC or spatially sensitive beamforming techniques \cite{alsamman_mmwave,han_thz,zhou_isac}.
This limitation motivates the use of deterministic propagation backends in protocol-level simulation.
